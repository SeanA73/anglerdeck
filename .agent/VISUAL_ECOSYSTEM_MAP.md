┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                              SUCCESS FORMULA                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Passive Income = (Subscriptions + Marketplace + Affiliates + Ads) × Scale

    Where:
    • Subscriptions = Recurring revenue foundation (20-25% of total)
    • Marketplace = Transaction-based income (30-40% of total)
    • Affiliates = Content-driven passive income (25-30% of total)
    • Ads = Traffic monetization (10-15% of total)
    • Scale = User growth × Engagement × Conversion optimization

    Key Insight: Start with ONE stream, perfect it, then add others.
                Don't try to do everything at once!
