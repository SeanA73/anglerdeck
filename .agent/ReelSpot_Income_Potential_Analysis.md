## Current App Overview

### What ReelSpot Does
ReelSpot is a comprehensive fishing companion app that helps anglers:
- **Discover fishing spots** with detailed information (1,000+ locations)
- **Log catches** with photos, species, weight, length, and notes
- **View real-time water conditions** and weather forecasts
- **Connect with community** of fellow anglers
- **Access marketplace** for buying/selling gear
- **Get AI-powered recommendations** for best fishing times and spots

### Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React + TypeScript + Vite
- **UI:** Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui components
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Storage)
- **Maps:** Leaflet for interactive mapping
- **Payments:** Stripe integration (ready to implement)

## Risk Assessment

### Challenges to Address

**1. User Acquisition Cost**
- **Risk:** Fishing apps have niche audience
- **Mitigation:** Focus on organic growth, content marketing, community building

**2. Competition from Fishbrain**
- **Risk:** Market leader with 15M users
- **Mitigation:** Differentiate with premium features, better UX, marketplace

**3. Seasonal Usage**
- **Risk:** Fishing is seasonal in many regions
- **Mitigation:** Annual subscriptions, global expansion, winter fishing content

**4. Data Quality**
- **Risk:** User-generated spot data may be inaccurate
- **Mitigation:** Verification system, community moderation, expert curation

**5. Monetization Balance**
- **Risk:** Over-monetizing could hurt user experience
- **Mitigation:** Keep free tier valuable, focus on premium value, no aggressive ads
